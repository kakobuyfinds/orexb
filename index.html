From discovery to decision

How to use your SuperBuy spreadsheet.

  1. Save the exact reference. Open the CAFANS product source and record the original seller link where available.
  2. Identify the variant. Keep the model, size, color and quantity together. A similar photo may represent a different option.
  3. Check agent compatibility. Review the available agent options and current purchase restrictions. A CAFANS listing does not guarantee SuperBuy acceptance.
  4. Compare measurements. Use the same units and measurement method for each candidate.
  5. Review the received item. Where warehouse inspection is available, check the selected option and request useful detail photos.
  6. Quote the packed parcel. Confirm available routes, packaging, chargeable weight and additional costs before shipping.

Before you commit

Three checks worth doing.

Measure for the fit you want.

For tops, compare chest width, body length, shoulders and sleeves. A flat chest width is not a chest circumference. Check how the seller measured the garment before comparing it with your own.

For shoes, distinguish foot length from insole length and confirm the size system. A familiar EU or US label alone does not settle the fit.

Ask for specific QC evidence.

Start with the correct item, quantity, color and size label. Then inspect visible marks, stitching, embroidery and fasteners. Request a close-up or measurement when an overview image leaves a question open.

Warehouse photos can document visible condition. They cannot establish authenticity, long-term durability or comfort. Check return terms while any available action window is still open.

Budget for the packed parcel.

Shipping may depend on actual or volumetric weight, depending on the route. Packed dimensions, packaging choices and restricted contents can change the available services and price.

Use current route quotes and destination import guidance. Avoid assuming a universal tax threshold, fixed delivery time or that the catalog amount is the amount you will pay in total.

SuperBuy spreadsheet FAQ

What is a SuperBuy spreadsheet?

A SuperBuy spreadsheet is a product research list used to organize source links, selected variants, prices and buying notes before using a shopping agent. This page is an independent guide with a CAFANS catalog selection.

Do these buttons place an order with SuperBuy?

No. The “SuperBuy spreadsheet” and “Buy it now” buttons open CAFANS. Product source references open individual CAFANS detail pages. Review the available source links and agent options there before ordering.

Does the displayed price include international shipping?

No. The displayed amounts are CAFANS estimated USD product prices captured on November 19, 2026. They exclude any additional delivery, agent, payment and import charges. Confirm the selected option and current quote at the source.

Can I use a product found in another agent’s spreadsheet?

Save the original marketplace listing where available, then check whether your chosen agent supports that listing and item. A wrapped agent link or seller album may require extra steps. Compatibility and purchase acceptance must be checked individually.

Are these products verified or authenticated?

This page verifies the catalog references used for the selection, not the physical products. It does not authenticate goods, inspect stock or certify seller claims. Product names and images are source material; confirm any important claim independently.

Why do the prices sometimes differ from the source?

This is a dated snapshot. Seller changes, selected variants, currency conversion, quantities and additional charges can produce different amounts. The current transaction quote takes precedence over the saved estimate.

How do I find more products?
